How to solve
  1. If fragments are composition-only, first work out each fragment's internal order using the cut-site rules.
  2. Scan for overlaps between fragments from different enzymes. Those overlaps are evidence that residues are adjacent.
  3. Chain fragments together from the most reliable anchor, such as a revealed N-terminus or a fragment whose placement is clear.
  4. Verify the full sequence: every revealed fragment should appear as one unbroken stretch, with nothing missing or duplicated.
